💡 What is poka-ce?

poka-ce is a simple, secure, and private personal finance application for your computer. It helps you track daily spending, set monthly budgets, and see exactly where your money goes – all without ever connecting to the internet.

Instead of sharing your financial information with servers or third parties, everything stays in a local, encrypted database on your device. Everything is private. Everything is yours. Forever.

🔒 Why offline?

  • 🛡️ Total privacy – Nobody can see your income, spending habits, or bank roll.
  • 📡 Works everywhere – Airplane mode, basement, or remote cabin. Anywhere.
  • ⚡ Always fast – No waiting for servers to respond. Instant data entry.
  • 💾 Permanent ownership – If the internet disappears, your data remains intact.

🚀 Getting Started on Windows

Step 1 – Download

Visit this link to download the application. This is the only official source for poka-ce.

⬇️ Go to Downloads

The download page shows several available versions. Choose the newest one for the best experience.

Step 2 – Run the application

Once your download finishes, locate the file in your "Downloads" folder. Double-click it to open poka-ce. When Windows asks for confirmation, click "Run anyway" or "More info → Run anyway" if SmartScreen appears. This is normal for uncertified open-source software.

Step 3 – Start tracking

The app opens immediately – no installation wizard, no account creation. You can enter your first expense within minutes. Your data is automatically saved on your computer every time you make a change.

🧭 First time tips

  • Add your accounts first (e.g., "Wallet", "Bank Card", "Cash"). Keep names simple.
  • Start with just three categories: Food, Transport, Fun. Expand later when it feels natural.
  • Review your weekly summary every Sunday – 5 minutes spent now saves real cash later.
  • Need help? The app includes compact help screens on every page.
